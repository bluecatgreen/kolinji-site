About Us

Kolinji Technologies is a technology consulting and services company offering a wide range of IT solutions to organizations to transform and augment their existing IT systems to a more efficient, modern, compliant and scalable architecture.

We bring 30 years of experience in various software development skills in the Telecom industry. Our leadership possesses vast technical experience in complex IT Systems, particularly in the areas of Service Delivery, Network/Service Assurance, and 4G and 5G Network Performance Management.
